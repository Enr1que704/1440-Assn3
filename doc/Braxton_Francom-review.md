This is a really good UML design! It is super easy to read what all of the objects are in each of the classes, and it is also easy to identify what each of the methods are, and what their appropriate orientation is, such as private or public. I like how they are presented, with the arrows showing how the different classes flow from one to the next, and they demonstrate that you have really thought about how this program is going to work. I haven't had a lot of experience with writing UMLs, since the ones that were required in CS1400 were very primitive, but everything that you have here goes perfectly with what we learned on Monday. It appears that you have considered each attribute in your class, and each class appears to be well thought out. They all have their purpose, and you should have no problem putting it together when it comes time to implement it all. Your class names describe perfectly what they are intended to do, such as deck including all the features of the deck, and card including all of the features that a card would require. None of your classes are closely related, so each one is good to stay as is. Just don't forget to update your UML as you think of new features your classes may require, or features that you don't actually end up need. Great job!
